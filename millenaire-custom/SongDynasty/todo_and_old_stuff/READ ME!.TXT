Put the song folder into AppData\Roaming\.minecraft\mods\millenaire\cultures
The Language folder inside the song folder, you should put it to \.minecraft\mods\millenaire\languages\en
Move the content of textures\song folder to your millenaire.jar\assets\textures\entity\
Move the content of goals to .minecraft\mods\millenaire\goals\genericcrafting
Move the content of quests to .minecraft\mods\millenaire\quests
Move "Song Materials 1.0" to \.minecraft\mods

Enjoy this plugin..
